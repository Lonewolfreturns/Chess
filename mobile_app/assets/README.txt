Optional custom art and audio
=============================

Everything in Wizard War Chess is generated in code, so this folder can stay empty.
Drop files here to replace the generated ones (rebuild the .exe afterwards):

assets/pieces/   2D piece images: wK.png wQ.png wR.png wB.png wN.png wP.png
                 and bK.png ... bP.png (transparent PNG, square).
                 Animated sprite sheets: wK_sheet.png (square frames side by side).
assets/sounds/   <name>.wav to replace a sound effect, e.g. boom.wav, thunder.wav,
                 move.wav, capture.wav, check.wav, promote.wav, victory_horn.wav
assets/music/    theme.wav - looping background music
